Measure first. **ccusage** shows where your tokens actually go. **RTK** and **Repomix** shrink what the agent reads from the shell and from your codebase, and **Serena** lets it read symbols instead of whole files. **Claude Code Router** sends easy work to cheaper models. **LLMLingua** compresses long prompts when you control the pipeline.
