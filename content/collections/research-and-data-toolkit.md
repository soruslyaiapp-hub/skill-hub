**MarkItDown** converts PDFs and Office files to Markdown. For the web, use **Crawl4AI** if you want to self-host, or **Firecrawl MCP** if you want a hosted service. **GPT Researcher** runs the whole research loop and writes a cited report. The **xlsx skill** handles the spreadsheet at the end.
