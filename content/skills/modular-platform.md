## What it does

One repo for the Modular Platform: the MAX framework for serving models, the Mojo language for writing fast kernels, and an OpenAI-compatible inference server.

## Why it is useful

- Replaces a pile of separate inference servers, pipelines and custom kernels with one stack.

## How to use it

Follow the getting-started guide linked from the README.

## Watch out for

- Mojo is a new language. Budget time to learn it before you write kernels.
