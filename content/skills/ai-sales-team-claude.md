## What it does

A sales team in your terminal. It researches a company, qualifies the lead with BANT and MEDDIC, maps the buying committee, writes personal outreach, prepares you for meetings, drafts proposals and produces PDF pipeline reports.

## Why it is useful

- Five agents work in parallel, so a full prospect brief takes minutes.
- It uses known qualification frameworks instead of vague scores.

## How to use it

Run the install script from the README, then type `/sales` in Claude Code to see the commands.

## Watch out for

- Research can include names and roles of real people. Follow the privacy rules in your region.
- Check facts about a prospect before you contact them.
