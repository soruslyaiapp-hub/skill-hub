## What it does

SGLang serves language and multimodal models with low latency and high throughput. The post quotes a production footprint of 400,000 GPUs and trillions of tokens a day.

## Why it is useful

- Scales from one GPU to large clusters with the same framework.
- A strong alternative when you outgrow a simpler serving setup.

## How to use it

Follow the installation guide linked from the README for your GPU and model.

## Watch out for

- Production serving needs careful tuning and monitoring.
