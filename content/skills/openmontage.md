## What it does

You describe the video in plain language. The agent handles research, scripting, asset generation, editing and final render. The project ships 12 production pipelines, 100+ tools and 700+ skill and knowledge files.

## Why it is useful

- A full video workflow inside the coding assistant you already use.
- Goes beyond animating still images.

## How to use it

Clone the repo, install FFmpeg and the dependencies from the README, then ask your agent for a video.

## Watch out for

- Many moving parts. Plan setup time, and watch the costs of the generation services it calls.
